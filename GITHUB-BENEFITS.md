---

## What GitHub Actually Is

GitHub is a free platform that stores files securely in the cloud.
Think of it like Google Drive — except it is built specifically
for publishing things to the internet, and it has been trusted
by the world's most serious organizations for over 15 years.

When you create a GitHub account and upload a file, that file
is backed up, version-controlled, and accessible from anywhere
in the world. You can update it anytime. You can share it with
anyone. And nobody can take it away from you.

---

## What GitHub Pages Is

GitHub Pages is a free feature of GitHub that turns your files
into a real, live website — with a real URL — at zero cost.

No hosting bill. No monthly subscription. No contract.
No "your free trial has expired."

Your website address will be:

> **yourusername.github.io**

That URL is clean, professional, and indexed by Google.
Every time someone searches for your business, this page
has the chance to appear.

## Why This Matters for Your Business

**Wix charges you every month.** If you stop paying, your site goes dark.

**Squarespace charges you every month.** Same story.

**Facebook owns your page.** If Facebook changes its algorithm,
your reach disappears overnight. If your account gets flagged,
you lose everything you built — immediately.

**GitHub Pages costs nothing and belongs to you.**

There is no algorithm deciding who sees your page.
There is no platform that can change the rules on you.
There is no monthly bill waiting in your inbox.

What you build tonight, you own. Permanently.

## The Three Things to Remember

**1. Free means free.**
GitHub Pages has been free since 2008. It is free because
GitHub (owned by Microsoft) uses it to attract developers.
That is not going to change.

**2. You own the content.**
Every word on your page belongs to you. Every image belongs
to you. If you ever want to move your site somewhere else,
you take your files with you. Nothing is locked in.

**3. This is how serious people build things.**
The website for the facilitator of tonight's workshop —
trust-lionel.com — runs on GitHub. Not because it is the
cheapest option. Because owning your platform is the right
approach for anyone who is serious about their digital presence.
