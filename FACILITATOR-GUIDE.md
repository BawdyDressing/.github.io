## Facilitator Guide — Lionel Mosley
### Thursday, September 24, 2026 | 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM EST

## The One Thing This Workshop Must Deliver

Every participant leaves with a **live URL** — a real, published webpage for their
business that they own, that costs nothing to keep running, and that they built
tonight using AI and GitHub.

Not a screenshot. Not a draft. A live URL they can put in their Instagram bio
before they go to bed.

---

## Audience Context

**Businesses in the room:** Phlebotomy, Catering, Daycare, Event Planning,
Homeschool, Housing for Veterans, Learning Academy K-8, Midwife, Notary,
Party & Event Planning, T-Shirts.

**What they share:** Every one of these businesses sells a service where
the customer has to trust a stranger before they spend money. None of them
are tech businesses. Most have a website that looks like a digital business
card — or no website at all.

**Tech comfort:** Assume low. Plan for someone who has never heard of GitHub.
Deliver something that impresses even the ones who have.

**Devices:** Smartphones and/or laptops. The exercise works on both.

**AI tool:** Claude (claude.ai) — Free tier. Every step of the exercise
must work within the free tier's limits.

## Run of Show

### 6:00 PM — Opening (10 min)

Open with the core thesis — say it plainly:

> "The most valuable page on the internet for your business is the one
> that makes a stranger trust you before they ever call you. Tonight,
> you are going to build that page. With AI. For free. And you will
> own it permanently."

Then land the hook:

> "SCORE will give you a mentor. LinkedIn will rent you an audience.
> Wix will host your website — until they decide to change their pricing.
> Tonight you are going to learn what it means to own your platform,
> own your data, and own your stack."

---

### 6:10 PM — Why GitHub (15 min)

Walk through the GitHub Benefits one-pager (hand it out or display it).

**Key points to make land:**

- GitHub is where 100 million developers store their work. It is free,
  permanent, and trusted by the biggest companies in the world.
- GitHub Pages turns a free GitHub account into free website hosting.
  No monthly bill. No contract. No middleman.
- When you publish here, you own it. GitHub cannot take it away because
  you stopped paying. It lives as long as your account does.
- Your URL will be: `yourusername.github.io` — clean, professional,
  and indexed by Google.

**The governance point — say this simply:**

> "Every tool I am going to show you tonight is free, open, and
> does not lock you in. That is intentional. As entrepreneurs, the
> worst thing you can do is build your business on a platform
> someone else controls."

---

### 6:25 PM — The Five Questions (15 min)

Hand out the Five Question intake card (or display it).

Give everyone 10 minutes to fill it out. Walk the room. Help anyone
who is stuck. The most common sticking point is Question 3 —
what makes them different. Push them to be specific. "I care about
my clients" is not an answer. "I am the only licensed midwife in
West Lakes who offers home visits on weekends" is an answer.

**Tell the room:**

> "These five answers are the foundation of everything Claude is
> going to write for you. The more specific your answers, the better
> your page. You already know all five answers — you live them
> every day. I am just asking you to write them down."

---

### 6:40 PM — Claude Demonstration (15 min)

Open claude.ai on your laptop. Display it on screen.

**Show them the prompt template — fill it in live with a fictional business.**

Use the Notary as your demonstration business — it is the most universally
understood service in the room and the one where trust is most obviously
the product.

Walk through:
1. Pasting the prompt into Claude
2. Reading the output aloud — let the room react
3. Adjusting one thing ("Claude, make the first paragraph shorter
   and more direct") — show them that they are in charge
4. Copying the final output

**Say this after the demo:**

> "Claude did not make this up. It wrote this from what you told it.
> You are the expert. Claude is your writing assistant.
> The faster you learn to give it specific input, the better
> your output will be."

---

### 6:55 PM — Hands-On: Claude (20 min)

Participants open claude.ai on their own device.

They paste the prompt template (from the intake card handout),
fill in their five answers, and run it.

**Your job during this time:**
- Walk the room
- Help anyone who cannot access Claude on their phone
  (pair them with a neighbor who has a laptop)
- Answer questions about their output — "is this good?" —
  redirect them: "Does it sound like you? Is it specific?
  Would your ideal customer read this and feel understood?"

---

### 7:15 PM — GitHub Setup (20 min)

**Step 1: Create a GitHub account (5 min)**

Have everyone go to github.com and create a free account.
Username advice: use their name or business name. No numbers.
No underscores if possible. This becomes part of their URL.

**Step 2: Create a new repository (5 min)**

- Click the + icon → New repository
- Repository name: `yourusername.github.io`
  (replace `yourusername` with their exact GitHub username)
- Set to Public
- Check "Add a README file"
- Click Create repository

**Step 3: Upload the template (5 min)**

- In the repository, click "Add file" → "Upload files"
- Upload the `index.html` file from tonight's workshop
  (have it available on a shared link — Google Drive or
  a CMET GitHub repo — so participants can download it
  to their device first)
- Commit the file

**Step 4: Enable GitHub Pages (5 min)**

- Go to Settings → Pages
- Under "Source," select "Deploy from a branch"
- Select `main` branch, `/ (root)` folder
- Click Save
- Wait 60–90 seconds
- Their site is live at `yourusername.github.io`

---

### 7:35 PM — Customize & Publish (15 min)

Now they personalize the template with their Claude-generated content.

For phone users: they can edit the file directly in GitHub's
web editor. Click the file → click the pencil icon → edit →
commit changes.

For laptop users: same web editor, or download and re-upload.

**What to customize in the template:**
- Replace YOUR BUSINESS NAME with their business name
- Replace YOUR HERO HEADLINE with their Claude-generated headline
- Replace the About paragraphs with their Claude-generated about text
- Replace YOUR SERVICE 1, 2, 3 with their actual services
- Replace contact information
- Change the accent color (find `#2C6E49` and replace with their
  color — tell them to Google "hex color picker" to find one)

---

### 7:50 PM — Share Your URL (5 min)

Go around the room. Every participant reads their URL aloud.

Have everyone open their neighbor's site on their phone.

This is the moment. Let it land.

---

### 7:55 PM — Closing (5 min)

**The three things to say:**

1. "You now own a piece of the internet. Not renting it —

2. "This page is the floor, not the ceiling. Every update you make

   This is how your website starts making you money."

3. "CMET built this workshop because we believe that the tools
   that power billion-dollar companies should be accessible to
   every entrepreneur in this room. Tonight, they are."

Close with the CMET program information — EBA enrollment,
the CMET Collective, next steps.

## Contingency Notes

**If someone cannot create a GitHub account tonight:**
Have them complete the Claude step and save their content.
They can publish the page tomorrow following the same steps.
The template and this guide are theirs to keep.

**If Claude hits usage limits on the free tier:**
This can happen with heavy group usage. Have participants
work in pairs if needed, or stagger their Claude sessions
so not everyone hits the API at the same moment.

**If the Wi-Fi is unreliable:**
Pre-generate a sample Claude output for each business type
in the room. Participants can use the sample to practice
the GitHub and template steps while Wi-Fi stabilizes.
