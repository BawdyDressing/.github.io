Made with intention

Bawdy Dressing is a Black woman-owned beauty and body care brand created with Black women in mind and open to anyone who cares about what goes on their body. Every product is handcrafted by me, in small batches, with intention and care.

I started Bawdy Dressing because our hair and skin have needs of their own, and I wanted care that nourishes without piling on steps or ingredients you can't pronounce. I choose each ingredient on purpose, and I want you to know exactly why a product is in your hands.

I'm not here to follow trends. I'm here to make quality body care you can trust, so that taking care of yourself feels like an enjoyable part of your day.
