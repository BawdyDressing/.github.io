# STEP 1 — Answer These Five Questions

Take 10 minutes and write your answers below.
Be as specific as possible. Specific answers produce
professional results. Vague answers produce vague websites.

---

**Question 1: What is your business name, and what do you do?**
Write one clear sentence. Example: "I run a mobile notary service
in Orlando that comes to you — wherever and whenever you need me."

```
[Bawdy Dressing is a Black woman-owned beauty and body care brand creating intentional hair and skincare products designed to support healthy-looking skin and healthy hair. I create products using carefully selected ingredients with a focus on quality, nourishment, and an enjoyable self-care experience. My brand is created with Black women in mind, while remaining inclusive to anyone who is intentional about what they put on their body. Every product is handcrafted by me in small batches, with intention and care.”]

```

---

**Question 2: Who is your ideal customer — be specific.**
Not "everyone." Who exactly do you serve? Example: "First-time
homebuyers in Orange County who need a notary for closing documents
and do not want to take time off work to visit an office."

```
[She is a Black woman, roughly 25–55, who is intentional about what she puts on her body. She wants healthy-looking, glowing skin and long, healthy hair, but she is not looking for a complicated 10-step routine or products full of ingredients she doesn't recognize.
She values quality, natural/plant-based ingredients, consistency, and taking care of herself. She is willing to spend a little more on products that feel purposeful and that she can trust.
She may be a busy professional, mother, entrepreneur, or simply a woman balancing a lot of responsibilities. Self-care for her isn't about perfection—it's about making time to care for herself.
And importantly, while Bawdy Dressing speaks intentionally to Black women, the products are created with the understanding that healthy skin and hair care aren't exclusive to one race or hair type. Your brand welcomes anyone who values intentional body care.]

---

**Question 3: What makes you different from everyone else who does
what you do?**
This is the hardest question. Do not say "great customer service."
Say the specific thing that is true for you and no one else.
Example: "I am the only mobile notary in West Lakes who is available
at 6 AM for early-morning closings, and I have never missed an
appointment in four years."

```

[Bawdy Dressing was created with Black women in mind and with an understanding of our unique hair and skincare needs, while still creating an inclusive brand where everyone can feel welcome. I focus on thoughtfully selected ingredients and products that nourish the hair and skin without creating an overly complicated routine.
I am not trying to create another beauty brand that simply follows trends. I want Bawdy Dressing to be a brand people can trust for intentional, quality body care—and I want my customers to know exactly why they are choosing what they put on their bodies.]
```

---

**Question 4: What is the one thing you want a visitor to do when
they land on your page?**
Pick one. Example: "Call me." or "Fill out my contact form."
or "Book a free consultation."

```
[ HOP THE COLLECTION What I want visitors to feel Within the first few seconds, they should think: “This brand is for me.” “These products look luxurious.” “I understand what they do.” “I want to see what she has.” “I want visitors to explore my products and find something that becomes part of their everyday self-care routine.”]


---

**Question 5: Tell me one real result you delivered for a client.**
A story, not a statistic. Example: "A family called me at 9 PM
the night before their closing because their notary canceled.
I was at their home by 7 AM the next morning. They closed on time."

```
[Myself and a few customers have seen changes in the moisture retention in our hair and skin. Soft healthy glowing skin. healthy shiny hair changes in growth. Moisturized beards less with irritation. And I don't go a day without several complaints on how good I smell.]

# STEP 2 — Open Claude (claude.ai) and Paste This Prompt

Copy everything between the lines below.
Fill in your answers where you see [BRACKETS].
Paste the whole thing into Claude and press Enter.

---

```
I am a small business owner and I need your help writing professional
website content for my business page.

Here is my business information:

BUSINESS NAME AND WHAT I DO:
[Paste your answer to Question 1 here]

MY IDEAL CUSTOMER:
[Paste your answer to Question 2 here]

WHAT MAKES ME DIFFERENT:
[Paste your answer to Question 3 here]

WHAT I WANT VISITORS TO DO:
[Paste your answer to Question 4 here]

A REAL RESULT I DELIVERED FOR A CLIENT:
[Paste your answer to Question 5 here]

Please write the following for my website. Use plain, warm,
professional language. Do not use jargon. Write as if you are
introducing me to a potential customer who has never heard of me.

1. A hero headline (one powerful sentence, 10 words or fewer,
   that makes my ideal customer stop scrolling)

2. A hero subheadline (one or two sentences that speak directly
   to my ideal customer and describe the result I deliver)

3. An About section (three short paragraphs — who I am,
   my experience and credentials, and what makes me different)

4. Three service descriptions (each one: a short service name,
   and two sentences describing what the client gets and
   what problem it solves)

5. A call-to-action sentence (one sentence that tells my
   visitor exactly what to do next and why they should do
   it today)

Make every word earn its place. Be specific, not generic.
The goal is trust — a stranger should read this and feel
confident enough to call me.
```

# STEP 3 — After Claude Responds

Read your output carefully. Ask yourself:

- Does this sound like me?
- Is it specific to my business — or could it describe anyone?
- Would my ideal customer read this and feel understood?

If something feels off, tell Claude exactly what to change.
Example: *"Make the first paragraph shorter."*
Or: *"The About section sounds too formal — make it warmer."*
Or: *"Add that I am licensed in Florida."*

You are in charge. Claude is your writing assistant.

# STEP 4 — Your GitHub Pages Checklist

Follow along with Lionel for each step.

- [ ] Go to github.com and create a free account
- [ ] Username: use your name or business name (no spaces)
- [ ] Create a new repository named: `yourusername.github.io`
- [ ] Set the repository to Public
- [ ] Upload the `index.html` template file
- [ ] Go to Settings → Pages → Deploy from main branch → Save
- [ ] Wait 60 seconds
- [ ] Visit `yourusername.github.io` — your site is live
- [ ] Edit the template: paste in your Claude-generated content
- [ ] Share your URL with the room
